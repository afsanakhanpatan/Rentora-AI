Rentora Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What can Rentora AI help me with?
A: Rentora AI can help customers find vehicles, check availability, understand rental prices, compare vehicles, understand rental policies, and get booking assistance.

Q: Which cities does Rentora serve?
A: Rentora currently has vehicle inventory in Chirala, Ongole, Vijayawada, Guntur, Nellore, Visakhapatnam, and Tirupati.

Q: What types of vehicles are available?
A: Rentora currently lists cars, motorbikes, scooters, and cycles.

Q: Can I ask about vehicles in a specific city?
A: Yes. Rentora can provide vehicle information for a requested city using the current vehicle inventory.

Q: What if I ask for a vehicle that does not exist in a city?
A: Rentora should clearly state that the requested vehicle is not currently listed in that city and can show vehicles that are actually available there.

Q: How is the rental price calculated?
A: The rental price is based on the vehicle's current price per day multiplied by the rental duration.

Q: Can I ask how much a vehicle costs for several days?
A: Yes. Rentora calculates the total using the actual vehicle's daily rental price and the requested number of days.

Q: What documents are required?
A: A valid driving license and one government-issued identification document may be required for the rental.

Q: Can I cancel a booking?
A: Yes. Customers should cancel at least 24 hours before the rental start time to avoid cancellation-related charges.

Q: What happens if I return the vehicle late?
A: A late return may result in additional charges.

Q: Can Rentora recommend a vehicle?
A: Yes. Rentora can recommend a vehicle based on factors such as city, vehicle type, passenger capacity, and rental requirements, using the actual vehicle inventory.

Q: Can Rentora answer general questions?
A: Yes. Rentora AI can answer general questions naturally. However, rental-specific facts such as vehicles, prices, cities, and availability must come from Rentora's authoritative data.

Q: What happens if Rentora does not have the information I requested?
A: Rentora should say that the information is not currently available rather than inventing an answer.
