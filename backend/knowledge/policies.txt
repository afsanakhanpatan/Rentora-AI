Rentora Rental Policies

Required documents:
Customers must provide a valid driving license and one government-issued identification document when required for the rental.

Cancellation:
Customers should cancel at least 24 hours before the rental start time to avoid cancellation-related charges.

Late return:
Returning a vehicle late may result in additional charges according to the rental conditions.

Fuel:
Customers should return the vehicle with a similar fuel level to the level provided at pickup.

Vehicle condition:
Customers are responsible for the vehicle during the rental period and should return it in reasonable condition.

Booking confirmation:
A booking is confirmed only after the customer explicitly confirms the rental.

Availability accuracy:
The AI must never claim that a vehicle is available unless the current Rentora inventory indicates that the vehicle is available.

City accuracy:
If a customer specifies a city, the AI must only provide vehicle information from that city unless the customer explicitly asks about another city or asks for alternatives elsewhere.

Pricing accuracy:
Rental prices must be calculated from the current Rentora vehicle price per day. The AI must not invent prices.

Unknown information:
If Rentora's available data does not contain the requested information, the AI should clearly say that the information is not currently available rather than making up an answer.
